STILL ORIGINALS
Memory of the Tide; Soft Geometry; The Space Between; Continuity. AI-generated images created for this experience. The virtual architecture is AI-generated, with interactive compositions of local artwork images. They do not depict a real institution or physical exhibition.

APPLICATION
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript by Orlando David, developed with AI assistance. No video, WebGL, remote API calls during a visit, user account or application server. The selection and language preference are stored only in the browser and may be cleared with its data.
